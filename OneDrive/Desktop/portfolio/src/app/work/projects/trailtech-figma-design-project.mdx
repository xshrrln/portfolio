## Overview

TrailTech is a Figma-based design project that allowed me to explore how a product idea can be shaped through thoughtful interface design and problem-solving.

## Goal

The main goal was to design a concept that feels practical, visually clear, and aligned with user needs while also showcasing strong design thinking.

## Key Focus Areas

- User interface design
- Design thinking and concept development
- Visual hierarchy and layout planning
- Product storytelling through wireframes and mockups

## Outcome

This project helped me strengthen my visual design process and gave me more confidence in turning an idea into a polished digital experience.
