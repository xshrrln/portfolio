publishedAt: "2026-11-13"

For me, art has never just been a hobby. It is how I understand and process the world. Whether I am working with graphite pencils and pens on a quiet afternoon, playing with vibrant acrylic markers, or putting together digital layouts, creating is where I feel most at home. Long before I wrote my first line of code or designed my first app screen, I was sketching stories on paper, writing poetry, and capturing little details of everyday life. That love for traditional textures and visual storytelling is the heartbeat behind everything I design today. Art taught me patience, close observation, and how to find beauty in simple things.
