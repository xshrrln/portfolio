People often ask how I manage to juggle heavy schoolwork like networking and database classes alongside UI/UX design, sketching, and creative writing. The secret is not a strict rulebook. It is about letting my creative side and logical side work together. When I start a project, I begin with simple, hand-drawn sketches and notes to figure out the human side first. Then, I move on to building clean, organized pieces. By letting logic and creativity feed into each other instead of fighting for space, I found a workflow that feels natural and truly works for me.
