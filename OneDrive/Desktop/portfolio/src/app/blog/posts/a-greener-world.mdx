Living and studying in Malaybalay City surrounds us with breathtaking natural views that remind us every day of what we need to protect. Building a greener world is not just a distant goal. It starts right here in our local neighborhoods, rivers, and communities. As students and designers, we have a role to play in protecting our environment. Whether it is speaking up for nature, cutting back on waste, or using creative media to spread awareness, every little choice helps build a healthier and more sustainable home for everyone.
