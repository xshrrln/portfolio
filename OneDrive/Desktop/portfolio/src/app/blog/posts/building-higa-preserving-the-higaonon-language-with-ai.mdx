Developing Higa has been an incredible journey in bridging cultural heritage with modern technology. Utilizing Flutter for cross-platform deployment, Marian NMT for text translation, and Google Voice Search ASR, we engineered a digital dictionary designed to keep the Higaonon language alive.

In this post, I dive into the UI/UX decisions made to ensure the interface remains accessible, minimalist, and deeply respectful of cultural context.
